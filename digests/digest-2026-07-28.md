# Pharma Trend Digest — 2026-07-28

## Signal Items

**1. Novartis pays $1.1B+ for a non-TOPO1 payload class (Myricx Bio acquisition)**
Novartis's $1.1B upfront ($400M more in milestones) for an NMTi-payload ADC platform is the real story here, not just "Novartis does ADC deal." This is a company that bet big on radioligand therapy essentially admitting the payload toolkit needs diversifying beyond TOPO-1 inhibitors and MMAE-class linkers. Watch for more acquisitions targeting *novel mechanism-of-action payloads* rather than "me-too" DAR/linker tweaks — payload IP is becoming the scarce asset, not the antibody.

**2. B7-H3 is quietly becoming the consensus "next" ADC target**
Three independent data points converge this period: GSK/Hansoh's ris-rez hit its Phase 3 ARTEMIS-008 OS endpoint in SCLC; GSK's Nuvalent acquisition is explicitly framed (per SEC 8-K) to build a lung-cancer commercial platform around ris-rez; and MacroGenics' vobramitamab duocarmazine (anti-B7-H3) posted final Phase 1 expansion data. Three different modalities/companies converging on the same antigen in the same quarter signals B7-H3 is following the HER2→TROP2 playbook — a broadly-expressed solid-tumor target where the next competitive battle will be payload/linker differentiation, not target selection.

**3. ADC infrastructure is catching up to ADC complexity**
Three separate but related pieces this period — a call for "third-modality regulatory science," AAPS's updated bioanalytical recommendations, and a CDMO-engagement strategy paper on ADC drug substance development — all point the same direction: the field is shifting from "can we make this molecule" to "can we regulate, measure, and manufacture it at scale, consistently." This is the maturation phase every modality goes through (cell therapy went through it ~5 years ago); expect more regulatory-science and CMC-standardization output over the next 12-18 months as approvals stack up.

**4. Conjugation chemistry is the live innovation battleground**
A cluster of technical papers — pyridine-based sequential conjugation platform, enzymatic conjugation technology review, real-time SEC-MS monitoring of cysteine-based bioconjugation, novel activated linker chemistry — shows the industry racing to solve DAR heterogeneity and process control, not target biology. This is where real CMC/CDMO differentiation will happen: whoever nails site-specific, real-time-monitorable conjugation at scale has a durable manufacturing moat.
